Take back your focus. Digital Habits: Blocker is a powerful, open-source focus tool that helps you block distracting apps and websites — on your terms. Built on more than 10 years of research into digital distraction at the University of Oxford, Digital Habits: Blocker is designed to reduce impulsive screen use without taking control away from you.

Create and customise blocklists, choose when and how long to block apps and websites, and hold yourself accountable with tailored override difficulties. 

Key features:
• Block Websites: Instantly restrict distracting websites across browsers
• Block Apps: Temporarily restrict distracting apps while a block is active
• Allow mode: instead of blocking specific apps / websites, choose what you want to 'allow' and everything else gets restricted automatically
• Quick start: start simple blocking/allowing sessions in just a few clicks
• Custom Blocklists: Create multiple blocklists for different contexts — work, study, evenings, deep focus
• One-Off or Scheduled Blocks: Start a permanent or temporary block instantly, or choose when scheduled blocklists become active and how often they can repeat
• Visual Timeline: See your blocklists at a glance on a clear 24-hour timeline (larger screens only)
• Pause & Resume: Pause blocks when truly needed — and resume automatically
• Stack Blocks: Run multiple blocklists at once without conflicts
• Override Protection: Choose how difficult it should be to cancel a block early — from simple confirmation to maximum-difficulty challenges
• Always Active: Blocks continue running even if you close the app

Digital Habits: Blocker is fully open-source and collects no personal data. It sends one anonymous count a day, with an ID that changes every month, so we know how many people use it. You can turn this off in Settings.
